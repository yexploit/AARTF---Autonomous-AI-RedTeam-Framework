## How to print

1. Open `ebook.html` in Chrome or Edge.
2. Press **Print** (or use the orange button at the top).
3. Destination: your printer, or **Save as PDF**.
4. Paper size: **A4**.
5. Turn **Background graphics** on so the cover, tables, and solution boxes keep their colour.

## What was solved

Every assessed item from the slide deck is restated and fully worked:
